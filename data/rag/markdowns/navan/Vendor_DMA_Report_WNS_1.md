# Summary of Double Materiality Assessment

# 1. About the Report

WNS (Holdings) Limited is dedicated to enhancing sustainability throughout its entire value chain. In FY 2024-25, we have undertaken detailed evaluations of both internal and external factors influencing the value chain, including their effects on the environment and society. By understanding the significance of potential impacts, risks, and opportunities, we plan to integrate these critical elements into our overall business strategy.

This is a summary of the Double Materiality Assessment for WNS (Holdings) Limited. This report presents an analysis of both financial and non-financial factors integral to our operations, informed by the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) guidelines. The Double Materiality Assessment guides companies to provide in-depth disclosures on their sustainability practices. This aims to bolster corporate transparency and accountability by requiring detailed reporting on how sustainability factors affect financial performance and the broader impacts of a company’s activities on the environment and society.

This assessment adopts the double materiality perspective evaluating both the influence of sustainability factors on our financial performance and assessing the environmental and social repercussions of our operations. This dual focus ensures that we comprehensively evaluate both the risks and opportunities associated with sustainability issues. The report meticulously details our approach to assessing these material factors, including rigorous data collection, extensive stakeholder engagement, and robust impact analysis.

This report provides details of our methodology, an analysis of our material issues, strategic responses, and a review of our progress and future objectives. This report underscores WNS (Holdings) Limited’s unwavering commitment to transparency and sustainable practices, offering stakeholders profound insights into our strategies for managing and mitigating sustainability related risks and opportunities.

WNS has engaged Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP to assist the management in conducting double materiality assessment. This Double Materiality assessment has been reviewed by the sustainability management team and the Board of Directors.

# 2. A Shift to Double Materiality

| Impact Materiality | Financial Materiality |
| ------------------ | --------------------- |
| Inside-out         | Outside-in            |

Impact materiality examines the impact of our operations on the environment and society.

Financial materiality evaluates how external ESG factors such as regulatory changes, climate risks, or social trends affect our financial performance and stability.

WNS is dedicated to conducting regular materiality assessments as we believe that these exercises are pivotal in defining our sustainability objectives and delineating the necessary actions to achieve a broader impact on the key issues that resonate with our stakeholders. Each evaluation deepens our insight into the impact we create and identifies where our business can effect genuine change.

This year’s assessment is conducted based on the double materiality approach. This new perspective allows us to evaluate not only the impact of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors on our financial performance but also to assess how our operations influence the broader environment and society. By integrating this dual lens, we gain a more comprehensive view of our sustainability landscape.

In our revised methodology, we have considered a diverse range of factors into our analysis, including the ever-changing legal landscape, geographical, demographical, and economical risks and opportunities, contemporary sustainability frameworks and standards, policy analysis, and thought leadership which are crucial from the WNS standpoint. We have also considered valuable inputs from key stakeholders to gain more insights on key risks and opportunities. Additionally, recognizing the dynamic nature of the sustainability field, we have also incorporated insights on key megatrends and sector-specific developments. This holistic approach has ensured that our assessment is not only aligned with current industry standards but also forward-thinking, enabling us to address emerging challenges and opportunities effectively.

We undertook the task of identifying and engaging with relevant stakeholder groups whose opinions are crucial for shaping our material issues landscape. We began by systematically identifying stakeholders both internally and externally, considering their level of involvement and influence on our operations. # 6. Assessment

Establishing a materiality threshold is essential for determining which issues are significant enough to warrant attention and action. The materiality threshold is defined by setting specific criteria that combine quantitative and qualitative factors. Quantitative criteria may include financial metrics such as revenue impact, cost implications, or required investments. Qualitative factors consider reputational risk, alignment with organizational values, and strategic relevance. The threshold-setting process involved defining benchmarks that classify issues as material if they exceed these predefined criteria, ensuring that only the most pertinent issues are prioritized.

# Financial Materiality Assessment

To assess the financial implications of identified risks and opportunities, we evaluated magnitude and likelihood/feasibility of their occurrence. For risks, this involves assessing potential direct and indirect financial impacts affecting cash flow, financial position, access to and cost of capital, including legal penalties, operational disruptions, or reputational damage. Conversely, opportunities are evaluated based on their potential financial benefits, such as cost savings or new revenue streams. The likelihood of these impacts is assessed using historical data, trend analysis and management judgment to assess their probability of occurrence. Scenario analysis is employed to predict a range of possible financial outcomes, providing a comprehensive understanding of risk exposure and opportunity potential.

# Learning And Development

Our talent strategy is closely aligned with business objectives and is designed to help us access, mobilize, and optimize talent in support of growth ambitions.

Built on four key pillars:

- (i) Developing a future-ready workforce,
- (ii) Strengthening a robust leadership bench,

This strategy positions us to adapt effectively to changing market demands.
