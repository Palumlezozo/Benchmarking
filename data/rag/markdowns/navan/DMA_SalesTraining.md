On your first day Enablement will walk you through the overview Deck that shows you what your onboarding plan looks like.

Throughout your onboarding you’ll have live sessions on different topics. These will be run by a mix of:

- Enablement
- Your manager
- A director
- Self-serve by you

In week 2 of onboarding you’ll go to bootcamp. This is a 4 day intensive training in person with hands on learning, exercises and roleplays.

You’ll complete a number of certifications over the course of your onboarding related to core SDR skills needed to be successful in the role.
