# Anti-corruption policies

Navan maintains an Anti-Corruption policy that reiterates our commitment to integrity, and explains the specific requirements and prohibitions applicable to our operations under anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws, including, but not limited to, the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act of 1977. All employees must confirm their compliance with the policy as a condition of employment.

# Whistleblower policies

In addition to reporting concerns to Leadership or Human Resources, Navan has a 24-hour hotline as an additional channel to report fraud, unethical behavior, or behavior employees think may be unlawful. Employees may access the hotline by phone or over the internet, and have the option to report anonymously or to leave their contact information.

# Diversity and inclusion reports/Labor practices

Blurb:

Navan abides by all equal employment opportunity laws, including exceeding minimum anti-discrimination and anti-harassment training obligations. Although Navan is proud to have a strong record of hiring and promoting a diverse workforce — including an executive team with multiple women and ethnic and religious minorities — we do not publicize an outward-facing policy or reports.

# Our Workplace

# Equal Employment Opportunity

Navan is an equal opportunity employer. We make all employment decisions based solely on merit. We provide equal employment opportunity to all applicants and employees without discrimination on the bases of race, religion, color, national origin, gender (including pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions), sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, age, military service, status as a protected veteran, status as an individual with a disability, genetic information, or other applicable legally protected characteristics. We prohibit any such discrimination or harassment. This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including, but not limited to, hiring, training, promotion, discipline, compensation, benefits, and termination of employment.

# Respecting Human Rights

We are committed to respecting human rights to ensure that our business plays a positive role in the communities in which we operate. We respect human rights by seeking to avoid infringing on the rights of others, and we expect employees, partners, suppliers, customers and governments to share this commitment. We abide by, and expect all employees, partners, and suppliers to abide by, all relevant laws and regulations prohibiting forced labor, modern slavery, human trafficking, child labor, and other practices that exploit vulnerable persons.

# Selecting Vendors and Suppliers

We require that our suppliers be as committed as we are to doing great work, providing equal employment opportunities, and following the law in all other ways. We select the best suppliers and vendors for the job, by carefully considering their proven track record, reputation for integrity, and other merits—not based on favoritism. We work to eliminate child labor, human trafficking, and other labor abuses in our supply chain and align with suppliers and vendors who share substantially similar codes of conduct and diverse supply chains. We do not conduct business with sanctioned organizations or individuals.
