# TC-SI-220a.1 - Advertising and Privacy Discussion

Describe the nature, scope, and implementation of policies and practices related to user privacy, with a focus on how you address the collection, usage, and retention of user information.

# Nature of Privacy Policies and Practices at WNS:

WNS has incorporated personal data protection as an integral part of its service delivery strategy. To do so, WNS has rolled out frameworks based on privacy standards. The WNS Privacy framework is built on ‘privacy by design' and ‘generally accepted privacy' principles, which in addition to the specifically applicable privacy regulations, incorporates privacy principles in other privacy regulations to ensure a holistic and consistent application of the framework across WNS.

Privacy commitments to User Entities are documented and communicated in the specific Master Service Agreement (MSA) / Statement of Work (SoW) with each user entity.

# The scope of privacy program includes corporate functions, client business operations and third-party services:

- Personal data of corporate employees
- Personal data of prospective employees
- Personal data of existing and prospective clients
- Personal data of clients' customers
- Personal data of third-party support staff and contractors

# Implementation of privacy policies and practices:

# 1. Collection of Information:

- Consent mechanism: Consent is obtained before collection and processing of the personal data.
- Purpose limitation: Data is collected only for specifies legitimate purposes and not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.
- Transparency: Privacy Notices, Cookie notices and Forms are used to explain what data is collected, why and how it is collected.

# 2. Usage of Information:

- Access Controls: Role-Based access to personal data.
- Data Minimization: Only the minimum amount of personal data necessary for a given purpose is used.

# 3. Retention of Information:

- Retention Schedules: Data Retention is governed by schedules based on legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.
- Secure Disposal: Once the data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

# 4. Data Protection Agreement:

WNS signs Data Protection Agreement (DPA) with all third-party vendors who process personal data on its behalf, including data processors and data aggregators, outlining their perspective roles and responsibilities with respect to the processing of personal data. The DPA also includes provisions to ensure that personal data is collected in a fair &#x26; lawful manner and obtains commitment to report actual or suspected unauthorized disclosures of personal information to ‘privacy@wns.com’.

# 5. Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) and Data Inventory:

Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) and Data Inventories are prepared and maintained by the WNS privacy team identifying the personal data collected, purpose and lawful basis of processing, categories of data subject, third-party vendors, safeguards, location and rights available to the data subject. The RoPA and Data Inventories are reviewed annually by the WNS Privacy team.

# 6. Breach Management:

Data subject / Functional head / Data Privacy team logs an incident by sending an email to 'privacy@wns.com'. Incident is also logged if vendors and other third parties with access to personal information notify WNS in the event of actual or suspected unauthorized disclosures of personal information.

WNS Data Privacy Team conducts investigations, necessary analysis and documents the details of incidents such as privacy violation, employee / group responsible, impact, severity, root cause analysis performed, and the corrective action taken within the 'Privacy Breach Management Register'. If the incident results in a breach, the privacy team shares a "Notice of Breach" to the affected data subjects, regulators, as well as other appropriate authorities and within timelines as identified within the Personal Data Breach Management Policy and Procedure.

# 7. Governance Mechanisms:

- Data Protection Officer (DPO) appointed for the organization.
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) conducted for high-risk processing activities.
- Regular employee trainings on data protection.
- Auditing and Review: On an annual basis, data privacy internal audit is conducted under the direction of the Data Protection Officer (DPO) to help ensure that privacy measures are in place. Gaps identified during the audit are documented in an audit report and communicated to the management in the annual Management Review Meeting (MRM).

# Disclose policy for disclosing data breaches to affected users in a timely manner

WNS has a comprehensive approach to data breach disclosure, ensuring transparency and timely communication with affected parties, including employees, clients and third parties. The key elements of our policy include:

1. Immediate Notification: Upon identifying a data breach, WNS promptly notifies affected individuals and entities. This includes employees, vendors, and any other stakeholders whose data may have been compromised.
2. Detailed Communication: The notification includes specific details about the breach, such as the nature of the data involved, the potential risks, and the steps being taken to mitigate the impact.
3. Support and Guidance: WNS provides guidance on the actions affected parties should take to protect themselves from potential harm. This may include changing passwords, monitoring accounts for suspicious activity, and other relevant measures.
4. Continuous Updates: WNS commits to keeping affected parties informed about the progress of the investigation and any new developments related to the breach.
5. Compliance with Regulations: The company ensures that all notifications and actions comply with applicable data protection laws and regulations, maintaining a high standard of data privacy and security.

# Describe your approach to managing identified data security risks and vulnerabilities

# Describe your use of third-party cybersecurity risk management standards

WNS has incorporated cyber and information security and personal data protection as an integral part of its service delivery strategy. To do so, WNS has rolled out frameworks based on leading security and privacy standards.

WNS has strong privacy practices and ensures that it complies with the requirements of various global/regional privacy regulatory regimes where it is supposed to act as data controller/data processor supported by detailed privacy frameworks, policies, procedures to address requirements of various privacy regulations, such as the handling of individual data including sensitive customer data such as financial information etc. WNS Cyber Security framework is compliant with ISO 27001 and 27002 standards. The cybersecurity framework is built on ‘security by design' principles and includes risk identification and assessment, threat hunting programs, cyber-attack preparedness and response strategy, cyber insurance program, internal assurance programs, employee and partner training programs as well as a robust certification and audit program. All these programs are overseen by a comprehensive governance mechanism that ensures that any residual cyber risk is either accepted, managed, or transferred appropriately. The framework is supported by cyber and information security policies and procedures, standards and guidelines to ensure consistency and completeness.

WNS's security architecture focuses on enhanced security for network perimeters of enterprise, secured connectivity to client environments and adequate network segregation for operating environments. WNS has also adopted Zero-Trust principles to reduce application exposure on the internet, establishing conditional access with MFA, enable role-based access for ensuring secured access for systems and applications.

WNS's cloud hosted environments are safeguarded by cloud workload protection, conditional access, and Cloud security posture management (CSPM).
